Strongest project

Real-Time Revenue Leakage & Customer Journey Intelligence

Simulated e-commerce data

End-to-end analysis of payment failures, customer-journey drop-offs, product returns and shipping delays to estimate revenue at risk and recommend what to investigate first.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • PostgreSQL
  • SQL
  • Power BI
  • Streamlit
Objectives, findings and recommendations

Objectives

  • Analyze the customer journey and conversion rates
  • Identify failed payments and unusual transactions
  • Analyze product returns and shipping delays
  • Estimate potential revenue at risk and generate business recommendations

Findings

  • Completed sales ₹870,887.01; net sales after refunds ₹754,415.20
  • 21 failed payment attempts; ₹151,989.12 potential payment risk
  • 54.87% delivery delay rate; 7 payment anomalies flagged

Failed-payment amounts are potential revenue at risk on simulated data, not confirmed loss. Anomaly flags mark transactions for review.

Business value: shows where payment failures, shipping delays and returns put revenue at risk, so teams know what to investigate first.

Recommendations

Python analysis produces recommended actions, presented in Power BI and Streamlit dashboards. The Streamlit app runs locally against PostgreSQL; setup is in the repository README.
